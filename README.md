# Growth Audit — YogaGo Flow

**Live:** https://hribeiro-adapty.github.io/yogago-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic YogaGo Flow already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in YogaGo Flow's own design. Only the sequence changes, never the art direction.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give them a second chance on close: the year at $59.99 next to twelve months at $19.99 | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Ask for the email after purchase, not before the first practice | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 03 | Ask one question (balance, strength or flexibility), then use it on the welcome line, the plan page and the paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Ask for notifications after the paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Put your 4.6 App Store stars and a review line on the paywall | CR +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for YogaGo Flow. Every price, rating, quote and plan detail comes from YogaGo Flow's own US App Store listing and in-app screens.
